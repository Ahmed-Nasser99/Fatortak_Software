Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Fatortak handles information on this website, in the public demo, and in the open-source application. Fatortak is open-source software. When you self-host it, you control the deployment and the data inside it.

1. Information we collect

  • Website requests: standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp and response status, used for security and reliability.
  • Contact and demo requests: the name, email address, company and message you choose to send us.
  • Optional analytics: aggregate page views, referrer source and a daily visitor hash from a cookie-less first-party counter. We do not store raw IP addresses in the analytics file and do not use analytics to identify individual visitors.
  • Public demo: demo data is sample business data. Do not enter real customer, payroll or financial records into the public demo.

2. Self-hosted deployments

If you deploy Fatortak on your own infrastructure, your organisation is the controller of the data in that deployment. We do not receive your database, invoices, employee records or credentials. Your hosting provider and your own backup and access policies apply.

3. How we use information

  • Operate, secure and improve the website and demo.
  • Respond to enquiries, demo requests and support messages.
  • Understand aggregate interest in the project.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use contact details for unrelated marketing without permission.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website may use essential cookies or local storage for language preference and basic functionality. The first-party traffic counter does not set cookies; it records aggregate daily counts and a salted daily visitor hash. Optional third-party analytics is only enabled when configured, and it records aggregate events rather than identifying you. You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser at any time.

5. Third-party services

The website links to GitHub, social networks and payment or contact services. Those services have their own privacy policies. When you open a link or complete a payment or booking, the third party processes the information you provide there.

6. Retention and security

We keep website logs and enquiries only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise them. We use access controls and encrypted transport, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you sent us by emailing info@fatortak.net. You can also object to processing or ask us to restrict it. We may need to verify your request.

8. Children

The website and demo are intended for businesses and professional users, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the product and website change. The effective date at the top will change, and material updates will be noted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@fatortak.net.

Fatortak is open-source software, not a law firm. This page describes our website and demo practices. It is not legal advice for your own deployment. If you process regulated data, review the policy with your legal and security teams.
